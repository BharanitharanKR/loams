Owner request, 2026-10-18: **"I want Postgres 16, 17 latest and plan for 18 and 19 support."** NF2 supersedes [NF1](2026-10-09-nf1-neon-fork.md) Tasks 24–35 (NF1e, NF1f), takes over Tasks 36–38, and brings forward Task 41's removal of 14 and 15 (D1107). Each task lists its files, the tests that must exist and pass, and its gate. Interfaces are fixed in §62. Before each milestone starts, its Task 0 checks the code as it stands and records rulings. **Postgres 19 is beta in every Loams artifact and document until Task 24's gate passes.**

**Goal:**
- 16 and 17 stay at upstream's latest minors within 7 days of each release;
- 18 is in beta, then supported, then the default;
- 19 is in beta after 18's beta, and supported only after 19.1 plus D1113's conditions;
- branches upgrade between majors through new timelines, and only when a user asks;
- the support window and the minor automation are in force.

**Architecture** (§62 §3, §6, §9): the work is split across three repositories.
- **`ostrium-labs/postgres`** gets the branches `loams/pg18` (from `REL_18_6`) and `loams/pg19`, each with our patch series ported once and later minors merged in.
- **`ostrium-labs/loams-postgres`** gets the Rust side at each major (`postgres_versioninfo`, `postgres_ffi`, `wal_decoder`, the pageserver), plus `pgxn/neon`, `compute_ctl` and the images.
- **This repository** gets the pin file's majors, a generated `majors.rs`, the API additions, the decoder and its fixtures, the stacks and the CI matrices.

## Global Constraints

- **Worktrees and branches.**
  - Loams: `~/Documents/Ostriumlabs/loams-wt/nf2-<task>`, one branch and one PR per task into `dev` (CodeRabbit reviews; merge on a green `CI required`).
  - The fork: worktrees of `~/Documents/Ostriumlabs/neon` under `~/Documents/Ostriumlabs/neon-wt/nf2-<task>`, on `loams/nf2-<task>` branches, with PRs naming `--repo ostrium-labs/loams-postgres --base main`.
  - `ostrium-labs/postgres`: branches through the REST API and CI jobs (`loams-postgres-merge.yml`). A local clone needs the owner's approval each time (NF1 Task 17 ruling 6), must be blobless, must not be in `/tmp` or `/mnt/Projects`, and is deleted afterwards.
- **Never** `git stash`, force-push, rebase `loams/*` or `main`, or delete a `loams-*`, `lp-*` or `neon-fork-point` tag. Use `git commit -s`. Every fork commit carries `Loams-Patch: LP-<nnnn>`; patches ported for a major carry `(ported from <sha>)`.
- **Builds.** Loams crates use the shared target directory, one cargo build at a time, touched crates only. The Neon workspace is checked locally with `cargo check -p <crate>` and headers from `scripts/pg2/pg-headers.sh`. Postgres, images, `neon_local` and `test_runner` run in the fork's CI. Never build in `/tmp`.
- **WAL-format rulings are never made inside a merge.** A change to what a record carries goes to the owner as a ruling, as 17.11's VM registration did (D1112).
- **Pins.** Images by digest; git dependencies by `rev` with a `loams-*` tag; sources by sha256; new versions at least 14 days old except security fixes.
- **Postgres 19 is labelled beta** (`-beta` tags, `beta = [19]`, compose profile `beta`) until Task 24.

## Review Focus

1. **A record of a new major is decoded wrongly or skipped silently.** Expected: an unknown magic, rmgr or info byte is an error. Tests: `unknown_page_magic_is_error`, `decode_all_rmgrs_pg18`, `decode_all_rmgrs_pg19`, `xact_stats_item_size_pg18`.
2. **VM state is lost at 19** (prune flags, `XLOG_HEAP2_VISIBLE` gone). Expected: never. Tests: `test_loams_vm_clear` at 19, `vm_set_by_prune_pg19`.
3. **Multixact members are corrupted at 19.** Expected: never. Tests: `multixact_wraparound_pg19`, `basebackup_slru_names_pg19`.
4. **A compute of one major attaches to another major's timeline.** Expected: refused. Test: `compute_major_mismatch_refused`.
5. **An upgrade loses data or runs without the user.** Expected: never. Tests: `upgrade_17_to_18_preserves_checksums`, `upgrade_failure_keeps_source_serving`, `upgrade_requires_explicit_call`.
6. **A beta major is offered without opt-in.** Expected: refused. Test: `beta_major_requires_opt_in`.
7. **A minor misses 7 days unnoticed.** Expected: the release issue escalates. Tests: `new_minor_tag_starts_release`, `beta_major_minor_tracked`.
