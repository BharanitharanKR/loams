# People of the Harbor Collective

The Harbor Collective is a small, fictional open-source group. This corpus was
written for the Loams GraphRAG example and is released with Loams under
Apache-2.0.

Ada Park leads Kite. She joined the collective early and still reviews most of
its storage changes. Ada Park works with Ben Ortiz on release planning.

Ben Ortiz leads Wren. He prefers small pull requests and long design notes.

Chen Wei maintains Tern. Chen Wei works with Dara Holm on the shared build
tooling.

Dara Holm maintains Heron. She also runs the collective's weekly office hours.
